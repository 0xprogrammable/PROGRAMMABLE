# Creator earnings

Classic creator rewards come from the buy and sell transaction fees selected at launch. The Programmable share is included inside each selected rate rather than added as a second fee. If a creator selects a 1% buy transaction fee, 0.9% accrues as creator rewards and 0.1% accrues to Programmable.

Rewards accrue in ETH and follow the recipient configuration recorded at launch. A creator can use the launch wallet, another wallet or a split between two and five unique wallets. Each beneficiary claims its own allocation, and a future recipient update does not move rewards that have already accrued.

The published public template policy describes a 0.2% total transaction fee. It assigns 0.1% to the template creator and 0.1% to Programmable. That policy is not active until public template intake and the exact payout route are activated.

Earnings depend on actual qualifying activity. A review, listing, template record or visible token page does not promise volume, revenue or a fixed payment.
