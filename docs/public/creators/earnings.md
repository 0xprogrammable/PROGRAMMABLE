# Creator earnings

Classic creator rewards come from the buy and sell fees selected at launch. The Programmable share is included inside each selected rate rather than added as a second fee. If a creator selects a 1% buy fee, 0.90% accrues as creator rewards and 0.10% accrues to Programmable on that supported market path.

Rewards accrue in ETH and follow the recipient configuration recorded at launch. A creator can use the launch wallet, another wallet or a split between two and five unique wallets. Each beneficiary claims its own allocation, and a future recipient update does not move rewards that have already accrued.

Public templates publish a separate 10 basis point creator share inside the intended 20 basis point template fee. That share is not active until public template intake and the exact payout path are activated.

Earnings depend on actual qualifying activity. A review, listing, template record or visible token page does not promise volume, revenue or a fixed payment.
