# Historical Stock Paired launches

Stock Paired was a separate launch model that created Uniswap v4 markets against allowlisted Ondo Global Markets tokens rather than ETH. Existing launches remain visible and documented, while new Stock Paired launches are closed.

The quote token, its issuer controls and the market route are part of each historical launch. A Stock Paired token is not an equity claim, does not grant rights in the referenced company and does not change the legal or transfer restrictions of the quote asset.

This page is retained for provenance and support. It should not be used as instructions for a new launch, and a historical listing does not imply that every external trading route or quote source is still available.
